# Governance

- **Who decides.** The maintainer decides, after public discussion in issues.
- **How decisions are recorded.** Significant technical decisions are written as Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr`.
- **Becoming a maintainer.** Sustained, careful contributions over time. Release rights are granted by the maintainer.
- **The name.** Forks are welcome under the licence, but must not present themselves as Catchword.
