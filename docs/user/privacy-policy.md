# Catchword privacy policy

*Draft, 5 October 2026. The Microsoft Store asks for this page. Before publishing it, the owner must fill in the contact address below.*

Catchword is a desktop app that searches documents on your own computer.

## What Catchword collects

Nothing. Catchword does not collect, store elsewhere, send or share any personal data, document, search, file name or usage statistic. It has no account and no cloud service.

## What stays on your computer

To search your documents, Catchword makes an index on your computer. The index holds the text of the documents in the folders you choose. It also keeps your settings, and small logs that hold no document text and no searches. None of this leaves your computer.

You can delete all of it at any time in **Settings, Delete all data**. Uninstalling removes it as well, unless you moved the index to a folder of your own.

## Network use

This version of Catchword makes no network requests. See [What leaves your computer](network.md) for the details, and for how to check this yourself.

## The Microsoft Store

When you install Catchword from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft learns that you installed it, as for any Store app. That is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement, not by this one.

## Children

Catchword collects no data from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here, with its date, before the app changes.
